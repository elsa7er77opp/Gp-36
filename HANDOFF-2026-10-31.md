## Decision taken on 31 Oct (agreed direction)
Ahmed felt the work was drifting. The diagnosis:
- 3 days went only into the public-dashcam pothole/bump detector, which is one slice of Task 4.
- More cleaning gives diminishing returns. The bottleneck is speed-bump data diversity.
- The final evaluation must be on the pod's own camera domain anyway.

**New direction:**
1. **Freeze the detector** as baseline v1 (YOLO11n above). Do not tune it further until new data arrives.
2. **Move to the system:** one live pipeline in which:
   - the camera frame goes to the hazard model (YOLO11n v1) **and** the COCO YOLO11n (people, cars), both running on the same frame;
   - their outputs go into a unified perception layer with namespaced IDs;
   - then a simple state machine gives SAFE / WARNING / STOP;
   - fusion with the distance sensor and IMU is added when the hardware arrives.
   This gives the supervisor a working demo.
3. **Data plan:**
   - ask the supervisor for EGY_PDD;
   - look for more independent speed-bump videos;
   - collect local pod footage when the lab car is available (the brief's Task 3 requires a local dataset).
4. **Project Outline** (8 Nov), after the meeting with the supervisor.
